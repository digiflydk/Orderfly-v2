# Central customer directory

The Customers page preserves the original customer records as its default tab, including brand and status filters, loyalty, last order, details, edit and delete. The directory is an additional tab. Both views read the same existing records; no customer records are deleted or migrated by this change.

The original records table now adds source badges, Games and purchases by merchant for each existing customer record. Booking profiles and Omnisend contacts can be filtered directly. Game-only contacts also appear in that table with a distinct status and a read-only profile link. The customer detail page shows all saved e-mail and phone aliases, per-address newsletter status, played campaign names, dates, merchant purchase totals and Orderfly orders across authorized merchants. A customer without a matching e-mail or phone keeps the original record and order history, with no inferred Games link. Cross-merchant links are candidate matches by e-mail or phone and are visibly marked as such. Merchant sessions receive only their authorized source records and never see another merchant's activity.

The Customers view groups existing `customers` and live `gamePlays` by normalized e-mail or phone, including the saved e-mail and phone aliases on an Orderfly customer. A superadmin sees possible contact matches across merchants. A merchant session receives only company-wide grants from `orderfly.customers:view`, and its rows use a brand-scoped identity key. The detail route resolves the key from freshly authorized records before querying orders. A guessed identity URL cannot bypass the tenant boundary.

The Customers page has two manual, brand-scoped sync actions for platform superusers. Omnisend imports one contact page per click and keeps the scheduled importer lease; when the backfill has another page, the UI says to click again. It never creates or sends a campaign. The booking action calls a private Supabase Edge Function, enqueues missing booking-customer jobs idempotently, then processes up to five jobs immediately. Remaining jobs stay in the existing worker queue. Both actions require a same-origin request and one explicitly selected brand. The booking bridge resolves the selected Orderfly brand through an active Supabase mapping before touching booking records. Deployment steps are in `docs/customer-manual-sync.md`.

Source documents remain in their original tenant collections. Orders, marketing consent, cookie consent, address, notes and loyalty are not copied between merchants. A Games opt-in is displayed as a fact about that play, not as permission to contact the person for another merchant. Test plays are excluded. Game-only contacts appear in the directory with zero sales unless a signed external purchase is attributed to that play. Existing checkout and booking customer records appear without a destructive migration or a backfill. The total includes paid native orders and paid external Games conversions with a unique play ID; refunded conversions and shared codes without a play ID are not assigned to a person. Native Games conversion events are excluded from the extra sum to avoid counting the same order twice.

Matching an e-mail or phone is a candidate link, not verified proof that two records belong to the same person across merchants. The UI calls this out. A future verified customer account or manual conflict workflow should replace the candidate rule before using cross-merchant data for automated personalization.

The existing customer detail view now places contact details, this record's order statistics, cookie consent and newsletter status by brand and e-mail address in compact cards. A subscription on one e-mail alias does not change another address's status. Order and feedback histories occupy full-width tables with independent date and merchant filters. Game plays have their own table with campaign, merchant, play date, opt-in and attributed external purchases; the duplicate merchant order summary has been removed. The top 10 dishes table sums item quantities from paid, non-cancelled Orderfly orders with positive net payment. It keeps identical item IDs at different merchants separate. External conversions without item lines are excluded from the dish ranking.

The histories query only customer and game source IDs from the newly authorized directory for the current session. Each order lookup constrains both brand and customer ID; feedback uses its own brand grant, and newsletter contact records use the brand plus normalized email hash. A provider unsubscribe or suppression overrides old consent. Pending consent and absent records are shown as such, rather than claimed as confirmed subscriptions. Marketing consent is never merged across merchants.

The current directory reads all accessible customer, order, game and conversion documents during a page request. This preserves historical coverage, but needs paginated projections and indexes before the number of records becomes large. Merchant-specific customer detail pages have edit and delete controls on the original scoped customer ID. Only the five primitive fields required by the editor cross the Server Component boundary. Booking identity resolution accepts either a valid email address or a usable phone number, and reserves whichever identities are present under the brand. The directory does not change order ownership or Firestore security rules.

## Customer list filters

Both customer views support text search across e-mail/phone aliases, brand selection, source type and an inclusive creation-date range. The customer-records view also filters by profile status and loyalty class. Customer types include booking profiles, Omnisend contacts, no paid orders, one paid order, two or more paid orders, or a Games signup without an Orderfly customer profile. Games signups have no loyalty class and are excluded when a loyalty class is selected.

The unified directory applies brand and date filters to the source records visible to the current session. Loyalty is evaluated on those brand-scoped customer records; candidate matching does not combine loyalty or consent. The date range refers to customer/source creation dates, not order dates. New bookings enter through the protected booking integration. A manual backfill can enqueue historical bookings that are missing a customer link, using only active brand/location mappings; failed identity conflicts are not retried automatically. Omnisend contacts enter through the authenticated importer after its enabled runtime mapping and `contacts.read` API key are provisioned. Contacts without paid orders appear in the no-orders customer type. The importer does not send newsletters, and a provider contact does not imply cross-brand permission.
